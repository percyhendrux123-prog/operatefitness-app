THE TWO OFFERS

PKFIT LITE — $20 ONE TIME

LITE is a self-guided digital product. It provides training, nutrition/macros/meals, and cardio tools. It does not include 1:1 coaching, direct messages, weekly check-ins, or coach adjustments. It is not a subscription.

PKFIT STANDARD — $250 PER MONTH

Standard is a recurring monthly coaching service. It includes personalized programming, weekly check-ins, and video form review. The subscription renews monthly until canceled.

FULFILLMENT

LITE access is provided through the PKFIT LITE web product after successful payment. Use the same email throughout the purchase flow. If access fails, contact support with the purchase email and transaction date.

Standard coaching begins after successful payment and completion of the onboarding information PKFIT needs to deliver programming and check-ins. Delays in supplying onboarding or check-in information can delay coaching delivery.

REFUNDS

LITE is a delivered digital product. Refunds are limited to duplicate charges or a verified failure to receive access that PKFIT cannot resolve after a reasonable support attempt.

Standard payments are non-refundable once the monthly service period begins, except for duplicate charges or a verified failure by PKFIT to provide the purchased coaching service.

Nothing here removes refund or cancellation rights that cannot legally be waived.

STANDARD CANCELLATION

To stop the next Standard renewal, send a cancellation request before the next billing date through the support contact below. Cancellation is complete when PKFIT confirms it in writing.

Cancellation prevents future renewals. It does not create a prorated refund for the current monthly service period.

PRIVACY

PKFIT uses purchase, access, and coaching information to deliver the product or service, provide support, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. PKFIT does not sell personal information.

Payments are processed by Stripe, which handles payment-card data under its own privacy terms. Do not send card numbers, passwords, medical records, or other highly sensitive information through social direct messages.

This static site does not intentionally set advertising cookies. Linked services may use their own cookies and privacy practices.

SUPPORT CONTACT

Primary support channel: Instagram direct message to @officialpercyk.

Include the email used during purchase, the offer purchased, the purchase date, and a short description of the issue. Never send a full payment-card number or sensitive medical information.

Fitness and calculator content is educational or research-oriented and is not medical diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice.
