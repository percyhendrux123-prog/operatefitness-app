# TWO SEATS — Creative Direction

**Status:** PRIVATE DRAFT — NO ASSETS PUBLISHED

## Visual idea

Two empty training stations. No contestants. No confetti. No giveaway graphics.

The emptiness creates the question: who is serious enough to occupy them?

## Hero image

A dark, real gym before opening or after closing.

- Two benches, racks, or marked floor positions separated in frame
- Percy centered between or slightly behind them
- Controlled overhead light
- Matte black, bone white, and restrained PKFIT red only
- Real texture: steel, rubber, chalk, sweat; no CGI
- No money, trophy, gift box, countdown clock, before/after mockup, or stock fitness model

### Hero typography

Top/small:

**PKFIT PRESENTS**

Center/large:

**TWO SEATS**

Bottom:

**12 WEEKS. COACHING FULLY SPONSORED.**

Footer:

**I’ll cover the coaching. You bring the standard.**

## Reel treatment

### Opening — 0:00–0:03

Locked wide shot. Two empty stations. Percy walks into center frame.

Text:

**I’M COACHING TWO PEOPLE FOR FREE.**

No dramatic impact sound. Let the sentence carry the weight.

### Body — 0:03–0:35

Alternate controlled talking-head shots with close details:

- Percy writing a plan
- Loading a plate
- Reviewing a check-in
- Empty station one
- Empty station two
- Phone/app detail without exposing client data

Keep cuts deliberate. No hyperactive captions. Emphasize only:

- **TWO PEOPLE**
- **12 WEEKS**
- **NOT RANDOM**
- **THE STANDARD**

### Close — final 4 seconds

Return to the two empty stations.

**TWO SEATS**  
**Apply for one of the two seats.**

## Story visual system

- 1080 × 1920
- One statement per slide
- Maximum 12–16 words of primary copy per slide where possible
- One visual anchor: empty station, Percy, plan sheet, or check-in screen
- Strong negative space
- No rounded SaaS cards, gradients, stickers, confetti, or prize iconography
- Application CTA appears only on the final slide and any reminder slide

## Progress documentation

Create consistency without making people perform for the camera.

### Baseline and final photos

- Same general location
- Same camera height and distance
- Similar lighting
- Similar clothing coverage, selected privately and used with consent
- Front, side, and back if within the approved documentation scope
- No filters, reshaping, or selective cropping that changes perceived result

### Weekly private process capture

One useful artifact per participant is submitted privately each week:

- A training set
- A prepared meal or planning moment
- A check-in insight
- An obstacle and the adjustment made
- A standard kept when motivation was low

The process should provide value even before a physical comparison exists. Public use is limited to the baseline, Week 4, Week 8, and Week 12 checkpoints unless the participant separately approves a specific additional item.

## Public narrative arc

1. **The offer:** Percy sponsors two seats.
2. **The selection:** readiness over popularity.
3. **The baseline:** where each participant is and what structure is missing.
4. **The Week 4 checkpoint:** the first real friction and how the structure handled it.
5. **The Week 8 checkpoint:** adherence, strength, routine, and approved measurements.
6. **The result:** observed change and the standard that produced it.
7. **The continuation:** what each participant will keep after Week 12.

## Tone controls

Use:

- Sponsored
- Selected
- Coaching seat
- Standard
- Execute
- Document honestly
- Individual structure

Avoid:

- Lucky winners
- Biggest loser
- Free transformation guaranteed
- Life-changing giveaway
- Insane results
- No excuses
- Limited-time frenzy
