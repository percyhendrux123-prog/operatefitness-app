# TWO SEATS — Participation and Media Terms Outline

**Status:** INTERNAL DRAFT FOR LEGAL/OPERATIONAL REVIEW  
**Not legal advice. Do not use as a signed agreement without reviewing it against PKFIT’s approved legal package and applicable law.**

## A. Participation terms

The final agreement should state:

1. **Parties and term** — PKFIT and the named participant; 12-week start and end dates.
2. **Sponsored coaching** — coaching fee is waived for the defined term; no cash value, transfer, substitution, or extension unless PKFIT agrees in writing.
3. **Scope** — individualized exercise programming, general nutrition coaching within lawful scope, check-ins, and adjustments.
4. **Excluded costs** — participant covers gym access, food, equipment, travel, medical care, and optional supplements.
5. **No guaranteed result** — outcomes depend on many individual factors; access to coaching is the sponsored benefit.
6. **Participant duties** — truthful intake, timely check-ins, communication, safe execution, and disclosure of material limitations.
7. **Health screening** — participation is conditional on onboarding, PAR-Q/approved screen, and medical clearance when indicated.
8. **Stop conditions** — participant stops and seeks appropriate care for warning symptoms or when directed by a qualified professional.
9. **No unsafe practices** — no undisclosed extreme dehydration, purging, starvation, drug protocols, or other unsafe actions attributed to PKFIT.
10. **Communication boundaries** — approved channels, expected response windows, and emergencies excluded.
11. **Withdrawal** — participant may leave the program; the coaching seat has no cash payout.
12. **Removal** — objective reasons and a fair notice process, except immediate safety or misconduct cases.
13. **Privacy** — coaching and health information stays private except for material the participant separately authorizes.
14. **Result integrity** — no manipulated measurements, altered progress images, or false claims.
15. **Legal alignment** — use PKFIT’s approved informed-consent structure; do not substitute an unenforceable blanket waiver.

## B. Media consent must be separate

Participation consent and media consent should not be hidden in one vague checkbox.

The media document should specify:

- Participant’s public name: full name, first name, alias, or anonymous
- Which assets may be used: images, video, voice, written answers, measurements, testimonials
- Which health or medical details are expressly excluded unless separately approved
- Where assets may appear: PKFIT social accounts, website, email, presentations, paid ads, or other named channels
- Whether paid-ad use requires separate approval
- Editing rights that preserve truthful context and do not fabricate outcomes
- Length of the license and whether it is revocable for future use
- What happens to material already published before revocation
- No obligation to endorse statements the participant does not believe
- Participant review rights for sensitive or health-adjacent material
- No sale of the participant’s identity or raw private records
- No guarantee that publication will create income, followers, or opportunities for the participant

## C. Recommended ethical boundary

Require documentation for coaching accountability. Request public storytelling separately.

Public participation is not essential to receiving or retaining a sponsored seat.

The campaign boundary is:

- Private weekly documentation needed for coaching is required.
- Public names, images, measurements, testimonials, and progress checkpoints are optional.
- Media interest is not collected or scored in the public application.
- A selected participant may later choose full name, first name, alias, anonymous identification, or no public use at all.
- Declining or withdrawing future public-media permission does not affect coaching, retention, or the sponsored fee.
- Only specifically approved categories, assets, channels, and durations can be published.
- Sensitive health data is never implied to be included by a general transformation consent.

## D. Truthful-results protocol

Before publishing any result:

1. Verify dates and source files.
2. Use materially consistent photo conditions where practical; disclose meaningful differences.
3. Verify measurements from the actual log.
4. State the participant’s observed adherence when used.
5. Avoid implying typicality from two selected cases.
6. Avoid health-cure or guaranteed-outcome language.
7. Preserve a private evidence packet for every published claim.
8. Obtain the participant’s content-specific approval when the material is sensitive or outside the standing media scope.

## E. Campaign rules disclosure

The public application terms should include:

- Sponsor identity and contact
- Application open/close timestamps and timezone
- Eligibility and geographic limits
- Two available sponsored seats
- Clear description of the coaching benefit
- Merit-selection criteria and tie resolution
- No purchase required
- No random drawing and no odds language
- Screening and onboarding conditions
- How and when shortlisted/selected applicants are contacted
- Response deadline and alternate process
- Privacy notice and data-retention plan
- Right to cancel or modify only for stated operational/safety reasons
- Governing law and required legal notices after review

## F. Data minimization

Public application:

- Collect only name, adult confirmation, coarse location, contact details, readiness answers, schedule/access, and yes/no safety gates.
- Do not collect medical records, government IDs, payment information, or detailed diagnoses.

After provisional selection:

- Move health screening and emergency contacts into the approved private client system.
- Restrict access to people who need it for coaching and safety.
- Set a retention/deletion rule for non-selected applicant data.

## G. Pre-launch legal review questions

1. Does PKFIT’s existing coaching agreement cover a term with the coaching fee sponsored in full?
2. Which existing informed-consent and media forms should be used?
3. Are remote applicants accepted outside Louisiana or outside the United States?
4. Does any jurisdiction require a specific promotion disclosure even though selection is merit-based and no prize is awarded by chance?
5. What nutrition-coaching scope language is required for each accepted location?
6. What applicant-data retention and deletion period will PKFIT use?
