# TWO SEATS — Campaign Operating Brief

**Status:** PRIVATE DRAFT — NOT PUBLISHED  
**Owner:** Percy Keith / PKFIT  
**Campaign type:** Merit-selected sponsored coaching initiative  
**Offer:** Two 12-week PKFIT coaching seats, sponsored in full

## 1. The idea

PKFIT will select two people who are serious about changing their bodies and rebuilding their personal standard. Percy will coach both for 12 weeks at no cost.

This is not a cash giveaway, random drawing, popularity contest, or promise of a specific result. Percy is underwriting the coaching. The selected participants supply the execution, communication, and honest documentation.

## 2. Why it works

The generosity is concrete: Percy gives two people his coaching and attention rather than an unrelated prize.

The exposure stays fitness-specific: every campaign asset concerns training, nutrition, adherence, obstacles, and documented change.

The seriousness filter is structural: people apply, demonstrate readiness, complete a short commitment screen, pass onboarding and health gates, and agree to the operating standard before selection is final.

The story is not “look what PKFIT gave away.” It is:

> Two people were given the structure. What they did with it became the proof.

Use one application link. Do not run comment-to-win mechanics, tag-a-friend requirements, follower requirements, or random selection.

## 4. What each selected participant receives

- 12 weeks of PKFIT coaching, sponsored in full
- Individual training structure based on goals, experience, access, and constraints
- Individual nutrition targets and adjustments within PKFIT’s coaching scope
- Weekly check-ins and coach review
- Progress tracking through agreed measurements and photos
- Accountability and plan adjustments based on observed execution and response

Not included:

- Cash or a physical prize
- Gym membership, groceries, travel, equipment, supplements, medical care, or laboratory work
- Guaranteed weight loss, muscle gain, appearance, or health outcomes
- Clinical nutrition, diagnosis, rehabilitation, or treatment

## 5. Program length and launch rhythm

Use relative timing until Percy chooses a launch date.

- **Application window:** five calendar days
- **Scoring and shortlist:** one day
- **Commitment screens:** one to two days
- **Final onboarding:** completed before public selection announcement
- **Program:** 12 full weeks from verified baseline
- **Required private documentation:** weekly check-ins and progress submissions needed for coaching
- **Optional public storytelling:** baseline, Week 4, Week 8, or Week 12 material may be used only when the participant later gives a separate, specific media authorization

Do not announce or identify a selected person publicly unless eligibility, onboarding, health screening, and a separate optional media authorization are complete.

## 6. Eligibility

An applicant must:

- Be at least 18 years old
- Not currently be an active paid PKFIT coaching client
- Be available for the full 12 weeks
- Have practical access to the equipment needed for the prescribed plan
- Be willing to complete weekly check-ins honestly and on time
- Be willing to follow an individualized training and nutrition structure
- Disclose injuries, symptoms, medications, pregnancy status where relevant, and other safety constraints during private onboarding
- Obtain medical clearance when the health screen or coach requires it
- Be willing to submit private weekly coaching documentation
- Public-media participation is optional, unscored, and never a condition of selection, retention, coaching, or the sponsored fee

No purchase is required. Follower count, social reach, race, religion, sex, disability, and popularity are not selection criteria. Any program constraints must be based on safety and the practical ability to deliver coaching.

## 7. The participant standard

The selected participants agree to:

1. Complete onboarding and baseline data before Week 1.
2. Complete every weekly check-in by the agreed deadline.
3. Treat prescribed-session completion as a coaching metric, not a moral test. Follow the plan as written or communicate early enough for a safe modification.
4. Track nutrition to the level prescribed and report misses honestly.
5. Communicate within 24 hours when a material obstacle threatens the plan.
6. Submit agreed progress documentation privately each week.
7. Decide separately, after selection, whether to authorize any public media; declining has no program consequence.
8. Avoid unsafe shortcuts, undisclosed drugs, dehydration practices, or unsupervised changes presented as PKFIT direction.

The standard is not perfection. A disclosed miss can be coached. Disappearing cannot.

## 8. Removal and alternate policy

Selection remains conditional through onboarding.

A seat may be reassigned only under the objective process in `09-HEALTH-EXIT-AND-REPLACEMENT-PROTOCOL.md`.

Grounds for review are:

- False application information
- Failure to complete onboarding or required clearance
- Two consecutive missed check-ins without communication, followed by written notice and failure to cure within 72 hours
- Materially unsafe conduct, harassment, fraud, or knowingly false public attribution of unsafe direction to PKFIT
- Withdrawal of participation

Illness, injury, family emergencies, and disclosed constraints are handled individually; they are not automatic removal.

Choose two alternates at final selection. Do not publicly identify alternates.

## 9. Selection workflow

1. Applications enter one private sheet or database.
2. Remove ineligible applications using objective gates.
3. Assign candidate IDs and hide social handles, profile images, and unnecessary identity fields from first-pass reviewers.
4. Score eligible applicants with the rubric in `03-SELECTION-RUBRIC.csv`.
5. Require reviewer recusal for a family, financial, coaching, or close personal conflict.
6. Shortlist the top six after a bias review.
7. Run a 10-minute commitment screen with each shortlisted applicant.
8. Use a second independent reviewer for finalists and materially close scores.
9. Select two provisional participants and two alternates.
10. Complete PAR-Q/onboarding and coaching consent.
11. Confirm the two seats privately.
12. Offer a separate optional media release only after selection; announce or identify a participant only if that person specifically authorizes it.

## 10. Exposure plan

The campaign earns relevant exposure through a documented process, not spectacle.

### Launch assets and optional participant assets

- Launch Reel: Percy explains why he is sponsoring two seats
- Story sequence: terms, who this is for, who it is not for, application CTA
- Selection announcement: optional and only after specific participant consent
- Baseline profile: optional; goal, constraint, and starting structure only within approved scope
- Weekly proof: optional; never forced or treated as payment for coaching
- Midpoint: optional; what changed in execution and identity, not only scale weight
- Final: optional; observed results, adherence, lessons, and next standard

### Content rules

- Never promise a transformation outcome
- Never invent or enhance measurements
- Never shame a participant
- Never expose health information without specific consent
- Never imply sponsored coaching creates any public-content obligation
- Show misses and adaptations honestly when the participant consents
- Label results as individual and variable

## 11. Measurement

Track the campaign as acquisition and proof, not raw reach.

Primary:

- Eligible applications
- Application completion rate
- Qualified conversations generated
- Paid coaching inquiries attributable to TWO SEATS
- Participant weekly check-in completion
- Participant prescribed-session completion
- Twelve-week completion

Secondary:

- Saves and shares on transformation updates
- Profile visits to application starts
- Email/DM opt-ins where consent is explicit
- Testimonial or case-study consent after completion

Do not judge success by views alone.
