# TWO SEATS — Health, Exit, and Replacement Protocol

**Status:** PRIVATE OPERATING DRAFT  
**Not medical or legal advice. Reconcile with PKFIT’s approved client documents before use.**

## 1. Safety order

No applicant is publicly announced as selected until:

1. Eligibility is confirmed.
2. The private fitness-readiness screen is complete.
3. Any required clinician clearance is received and reviewed for practical restrictions.
4. PKFIT confirms it can safely deliver coaching within scope.
5. Participation and media documents are complete.

A private safety deferral is never described publicly as a failure, disqualification, or lack of discipline.

## 2. Public application health gate

Collect only:

> Do you know of any injury, symptom, diagnosis, medication, pregnancy, recent procedure, or clinician instruction that could affect exercise or nutrition coaching? Yes / No / Prefer to discuss privately.

Do not collect details in the public form or DMs.

## 3. Finalist workflow

- **No:** Complete the approved private health screen before final selection.
- **Yes / Prefer private:** Send the secure onboarding route. Do not ask for details in social DMs.
- Review the approved health screen for coaching constraints; do not diagnose.
- If the screen or observed symptoms indicate clearance is required, mark the seat pending.
- Accept clinician documentation only through the approved secure route.
- Record only the practical clearance status and restrictions needed for coaching.

## 4. Scope decision

Percy records one of four states:

- **Cleared within coaching scope**
- **Cleared with documented modifications**
- **Pending clinician clearance**
- **Cannot safely serve within current scope — deferred privately**

No public announcement occurs in pending or deferred states.

## 5. In-program stop/escalation

Stop the affected activity and direct the participant to appropriate care when warning symptoms, an acute injury, clinician instruction, or another material safety concern arises. PKFIT does not provide emergency care, diagnosis, rehabilitation, or treatment.

A health-related plan modification is not noncompliance.

## 6. Attendance and communication process

Training-session completion is a coaching metric, not an automatic removal threshold.

When a participant misses a weekly check-in:

1. Send one private operational reminder through the approved coaching channel.
2. If a second consecutive check-in is missed without communication, send a written notice identifying the missed obligations and a 72-hour cure window.
3. If the participant responds, document the obstacle and agree on a safe recovery or modification.
4. If no response arrives within 72 hours, Percy may close the sponsored seat by private written notice.

Illness, injury, family emergency, work disruption, disability accommodation, or an agreed plan modification does not trigger removal by itself.

## 7. Immediate suspension grounds

Percy may pause coaching immediately while reviewing:

- A credible acute safety threat
- Harassment or threats
- Fraud or materially false application information
- Deliberate falsification of progress evidence
- Knowingly attributing unsafe instructions to PKFIT that PKFIT did not provide

Except when immediate separation is necessary for safety, provide the participant the observed concern and a reasonable opportunity to respond before final removal.

## 8. Exit communication

All withdrawal, deferral, and removal notices are private. Public content says only what the participant separately approves. PKFIT does not invite speculation about a health issue, family event, conflict, or departure.

On exit:

- Stop future coaching access according to the signed terms
- Preserve required records securely
- Process future-use media withdrawal under the media agreement
- Do not delete evidence needed to resolve a dispute or comply with law
- Do not publish an “explanation” without separate approval

## 9. Replacement rule

- Maintain two private alternates through the end of Week 1.
- If a provisional participant cannot start, or exits before completing Week 1, an alternate may begin a new full 12-week term with a new baseline and their own documents.
- After Week 1 is completed, do not insert an alternate into the remaining term. Continue with the remaining participant or close that seat privately.
- Alternates receive no public identification unless they become a participant and complete all gates.

## 10. Decision record

For every deferral, modification, warning, removal, or replacement, record:

- Date
- Observed trigger
- Applicable rule
- Communication sent
- Cure/response deadline if applicable
- Decision owner
- Final state
- Evidence handle

Do not record speculative diagnosis or moral judgment.
