# TWO SEATS — Media Consent Specification

**Status:** INTERNAL SPECIFICATION — NOT A SIGNABLE AGREEMENT  
**Requires reconciliation with PKFIT’s approved legal package before use.**

## Optional choices

The final media document must let each participant choose:

### Public identity

- Full name
- First name only
- Alias
- Anonymous

The participant may also choose no public use. All choices have no effect on selection, retention, coaching, or the sponsored fee.

### Optional checkpoint assets

If a participant elects public storytelling at baseline, Week 4, Week 8, or Week 12, identify exactly which assets may be used:

- Progress image
- Training video
- Audio/video reflection
- Written quote
- Non-sensitive adherence summary
- Approved measurements

Private weekly submissions are excluded by default.

### Channels

Use separate choices for:

- PKFIT organic social accounts
- PKFIT website
- PKFIT email
- Printed or live presentation
- Paid advertising
- Third-party publication

**Default campaign scope:** no public use. PKFIT organic social, website, email, paid advertising, and third-party publication each require separate, specific opt-in and are not required for the sponsored seat.

## Truthful editing

PKFIT may crop, caption, subtitle, and edit for length only when the edit preserves truthful context and does not fabricate outcomes, alter physique appearance, misstate adherence, or imply a health result.

No filters, body reshaping, synthetic replacement, undisclosed compositing, or misleading photo-condition manipulation.

## Sensitive content review

Before publication, the participant must approve any item containing:

- Exact weight, body-fat estimate, or body measurements
- Health, injury, medication, pregnancy, or clinician information
- A personal story not already approved in the standing content scope
- A direct endorsement or testimonial
- A reason for absence, modification, deferral, or exit

Silence is not approval.

## Duration and withdrawal

The final agreement must state:

- License start date
- License duration
- How the participant withdraws future-use permission
- The contact and response window
- What PKFIT will stop using after withdrawal
- What happens to material already published or lawfully distributed
- Whether archival evidence is retained privately

Recommended operational rule: withdrawal stops new publication and paid use after processing; PKFIT removes controllable organic assets within the stated window when practical, while acknowledging that third-party shares, screenshots, caches, and already distributed material may persist.

## No coercive expansion

The sponsored seat requires no public content. Every public checkpoint, testimonial, paid-ad use, raw private record, or post-program endorsement requires separate voluntary approval and cannot be demanded as repayment for coaching.

## Participant copy and record

Provide the participant a completed copy. Store:

- Signed version and timestamp
- Identity choice
- Asset/category choices
- Channel choices
- Sensitive-content approvals
- Withdrawal requests and processing evidence

Do not store the media agreement in a public content folder.
