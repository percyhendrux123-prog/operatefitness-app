## 7. Selection

Eligible applications are assigned candidate IDs. First-pass reviewers do not receive optional social handles or profile images.

Applications are scored on:

- Readiness evidence: 30 points
- Execution capacity: 25 points
- Goal clarity: 15 points
- Schedule and environment: 15 points
- Coachability: 15 points

Shortlisted applicants complete the same core commitment screen. Close scores and finalists receive a second review. Conflicts of interest require reviewer recusal. Ties resolve by readiness evidence, execution capacity, coachability, and then independent rescoring—in that order. No random tie-break is used.
