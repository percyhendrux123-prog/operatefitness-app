# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with a Netlify Forms submission contract because the campaign requires a fast, inspectable mobile application and private Netlify response storage.

## Users

Louisiana adults age 18 or older who want twelve weeks of individualized PKFIT coaching and can complete the stated training, weekly check-in, safety, and documentation requirements.

## Product Purpose

Let eligible applicants understand the sponsored coaching offer, its obligations, privacy limits, schedule, and selection method, then submit a minimized mobile application. Success is a valid application that reaches the private campaign response destination without collecting detailed health information.

## Positioning

Two merit-selected coaching seats. PKFIT sponsors the coaching fee in full; the applicant brings the standard required to execute for twelve weeks.

## Operating Context

Applications open after approved publication on September 13, 2026 and close Monday, September 14, 2026 at 8:00 p.m. CDT. Blinded review and standardized screens occur September 15. Provisional selections must complete health, terms, consent, and account onboarding by September 16. Coaching begins Monday, September 21, 2026 and the final day is Sunday, December 13, 2026.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Exactly two sponsored coaching seats.
- Louisiana residents only; adults 18 or older.
- Current active paid PKFIT clients are ineligible.
- Selection is based on eligibility, readiness, coachability, and practical ability to complete the program; it is not random.
- The public form collects a safety gate only, never diagnoses, medication details, records, payment data, or detailed health information.
- Final participation remains conditional on private health screening and medical clearance when indicated.
- The coaching fee is sponsored; gym access, food, equipment, healthcare, travel, and optional supplements are not included.
- No result is guaranteed.
- Public progress checkpoints are optional, unscored, and require a later separately executed, asset- and channel-specific media consent; declining has no program consequence.
- No bundled marketing consent and no automatic messages.

## Brand Commitments

PKFIT Quiet Assassin voice: calm, precise, controlled, mechanism-first, masculine restraint. Near-black field with restrained brass/gold, real gym material, no hype, emojis, hashtags, fake urgency, or transformation theater.

## Evidence on Hand

- Campaign decisions and rules under this directory.
- Real empty-gym source photograph at `assets/source/two-seats-gym.jpg`; no person is visible and it must not be presented as participant proof.
- Existing PKFIT coaching systems and health-screen path; no campaign result or applicant count exists yet.

## Product Principles

1. Explain the standard before asking for the application.
2. Minimize public data collection.
3. Preserve health, consent, and human-selection gates under a compressed timeline.
4. Make the deadline exact rather than theatrically urgent.
5. Never confuse sponsored access with a promised outcome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The form must be keyboard-operable, mobile-first, screen-reader labeled, high-contrast, usable at 200% zoom, and must not penalize an applicant for declining public media or flagging a health/safety constraint for private review.
