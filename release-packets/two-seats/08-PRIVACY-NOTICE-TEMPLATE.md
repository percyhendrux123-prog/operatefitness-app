### Purpose

PKFIT collects application information to:

- Verify eligibility
- Evaluate readiness and operational fit
- Contact shortlisted and selected applicants
- Conduct private safety/onboarding steps for finalists
- Administer the sponsored coaching initiative
- Document selection and consent decisions

Application contact information is not used for unrelated marketing unless the applicant separately opts in.

### Data collected at application stage

- Name and adult confirmation
- Coarse location
- One primary contact route and preferred channel
- Optional social handle, hidden from first-pass scoring
- Goal, readiness, schedule, training access, and constraint answers
- Yes/no/prefer-private safety gate
- Required acknowledgments and typed-name attestation
- Technical submission metadata produced by **[APPLICATION PROCESSOR]**

Do not collect detailed diagnoses, medical records, emergency contacts, payment data, government identification, or account credentials in the public form.

### Who can access it

- Percy Keith as campaign owner
- Named PKFIT operations personnel who need access for application administration
- **[APPLICATION PROCESSOR]** as the form/data service

A reviewer conducting first-pass scoring receives a candidate ID and redacted answers without social handles, profile photos, or unnecessary identity fields.

### Safety follow-up

A yes or prefer-private safety response does not collect details through social media or the public form. If shortlisted, the applicant receives **[SECURE ONBOARDING METHOD]** for the approved private health screen. Percy does not diagnose conditions. When the screen indicates clearance, participation remains pending until acceptable clinician clearance is received through the secure method.

### Retention

- Non-selected application records: delete within 30 days after the final selection decision or campaign close, whether zero, one, or two seats are filled
- Scoring audit record: retain **[APPROVED PERIOD]** with candidate ID and factual decision rationale; remove unnecessary contact and health-gate data
- Selected participant records: move only necessary information into the approved private client system and follow its retention policy
- Withdrawal/deletion request: process through **[APPROVED PRIVACY EMAIL]**, subject to documented legal or dispute-preservation needs

### Security and incidents

- Limit access to named roles
- Do not export health answers into social DMs, shared content folders, or marketing lists
- Do not place applicant data in model prompts outside the approved private workflow
- If unauthorized access or disclosure is suspected: stop access, preserve evidence, notify Percy, assess affected data, and follow counsel/provider notification requirements
