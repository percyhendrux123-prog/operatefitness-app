# TWO SEATS — Results Evidence Protocol

**Status:** PRIVATE OPERATING DRAFT

## Accountability

- **Evidence owner:** Percy Keith or one explicitly named PKFIT operator
- **Claim approver:** Percy Keith
- **Participant review:** required for sensitive, measurement-based, testimonial, or health-adjacent content
- **Public publisher:** Percy or another specifically approved human action

## Evidence packet per participant

Maintain a restricted folder or approved client record containing:

- Signed participation and media documents
- Verified baseline date
- Original baseline assets
- Week 4, Week 8, and Week 12 source assets
- Check-in and prescribed-session records used in public claims
- Measurement source and date
- Caption/script version shown to participant when review is required
- Participant approval record
- Published URL and capture
- Corrections or withdrawal requests

## Verification before each public checkpoint

1. Confirm participant identity and checkpoint date.
2. Compare against original source assets, not social exports.
3. Verify measurements from the actual private record.
4. Confirm photo conditions and disclose material differences.
5. Confirm the claim describes individual observed results.
6. Remove any unsupported implication of typicality, guarantee, diagnosis, or treatment.
7. Confirm the asset and channel are authorized by the current media record.
8. Obtain participant approval where required.
9. Obtain Percy’s exact publication approval.
10. Publish, then read back the live post and store its evidence handle.

## Storage controls

- Restrict raw progress and health-adjacent files to coaching/evidence roles
- Do not place raw files in public production folders
- Do not send health details or unredacted applications to unapproved models
- Preserve original timestamps and metadata where available
- Do not overwrite source assets with edited versions
- Use candidate/participant IDs in reviewer materials where practical

## Correction protocol

If a published claim or asset is wrong:

1. Stop further reuse.
2. Capture the live error as evidence.
3. Notify Percy and the participant privately.
4. Prepare correction/removal options.
5. Act only with the required approval unless immediate removal is necessary for privacy or safety under the signed terms.
6. Record the final correction and live verification.

## Final result language

Use:

> Over 12 weeks, [approved identity] completed [verified activity], changed [verified measurement if approved], and built [observed repeatable behavior]. These are this participant’s individual results and do not guarantee another person’s outcome.

Do not use:

- Guaranteed transformation
- Typical results without evidence
- Cure, treatment, reversal, or clinical claims
- Manipulated before/after comparisons
- A testimonial the participant did not approve
