# TWO SEATS — Selection Rubric

**Status:** PRIVATE INTERNAL TOOL

Score only after automatic eligibility gates pass. Use application evidence and the standardized commitment screen. Do not score appearance, follower count, popularity, protected characteristics, social reach, public identity choice, or how emotionally dramatic a story sounds.

Assign a candidate ID before first-pass scoring. Hide optional social handles, profile photos, and unnecessary identity fields. A reviewer must recuse from any family, financial, coaching, or close personal conflict. Use a second independent reviewer for finalists and materially close scores.

Public-media interest is not collected or scored. Declining public use cannot affect eligibility, ranking, selection, retention, coaching, or the sponsored fee.

## 1. Readiness evidence — 0 to 30

- **0–6:** Desire stated, but no recent evidence of sustained follow-through
- **7–14:** Some follow-through; examples are vague or shorter than eight weeks
- **15–23:** Specific recent example with obstacles and recovery after misses
- **24–30:** Strong, specific evidence of sustained execution plus a realistic reason this 12-week window is workable

## 2. Execution capacity — 0 to 25

- **0–6:** No reliable training window or usable environment
- **7–13:** Access exists, but schedule is mostly aspirational
- **14–20:** Concrete weekly windows, equipment access, and basic contingency plan
- **21–25:** Concrete schedule, environment support, and credible fallback for predictable disruptions

## 3. Goal clarity — 0 to 15

- **0–3:** Vague or unsafe outcome expectation
- **4–7:** General physical goal without a clear personal meaning
- **8–11:** Clear physical goal and a realistic reason it matters
- **12–15:** Clear goal, meaningful identity stake, and realistic expectations for 12 weeks

## 4. Schedule and environment — 0 to 15

- **0–3:** Major unaddressed conflict makes completion unlikely
- **4–7:** Constraints identified without a plan
- **8–11:** Constraints identified with workable support or adaptations
- **12–15:** Schedule, household/work environment, and disruption plan are specific and credible

## 5. Coachability — 0 to 15

- **0–3:** Expects perfection, resists feedback, or externalizes every prior miss
- **4–7:** Open to coaching but vague about response to setbacks
- **8–11:** Can name difficult feedback and describes honest recovery after misses
- **12–15:** Demonstrates autonomy, honest self-assessment, and willingness to communicate before disappearing

## Thresholds

- **Below 60:** Do not shortlist
- **60–74:** Hold only if the applicant pool is limited and the screen could resolve a specific uncertainty
- **75–84:** Shortlist eligible
- **85–100:** Strong shortlist

A high score does not override a safety or eligibility gate.

## Tie resolution

Resolve ties in this order:

1. Stronger readiness evidence
2. Stronger execution capacity
3. Stronger coachability
4. If still tied, a second reviewer independently rescoring the tied applications

Do not use a random drawing, social engagement, appearance, identity format, or perceived marketability to break a tie.

## Bias check before final selection

For each top candidate, ask:

- Would I score this application the same without a profile photo or social handle?
- Am I rewarding a dramatic story instead of credible execution?
- Am I penalizing shift work, disability, limited equipment, or privacy needs when a safe adaptation is practical?
- Am I favoring full-name publicity over alias or anonymous participation?
- Is the expected visual transformation being valued more than coachability and completion probability?
- Did every shortlisted applicant receive the same core screen questions?

Document the reason for each final decision in one or two factual sentences.

## Final designation

- Two provisional selections
- Two private alternates
- Final status only after onboarding, health screening, coaching terms, and media terms are complete
