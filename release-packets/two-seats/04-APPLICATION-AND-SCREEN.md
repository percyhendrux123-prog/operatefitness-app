# TWO SEATS — Application and Screening

**Status:** PRIVATE DRAFT — DO NOT COLLECT RESPONSES UNTIL APPROVED

## Application introduction

PKFIT is selecting two adults for 12 weeks of coaching with the coaching fee sponsored in full. Selection is based on eligibility, readiness, coachability, and practical ability to complete the program.

Selected participants must complete private weekly coaching documentation. Public names, images, measurements, testimonials, and progress checkpoints are optional, are not collected or scored in the application, and require a later separate, specific media release.

There is no purchase, cash prize, random drawing, or guaranteed result. Do not include medical records, financial information, passwords, or other sensitive documents in this application.

## Public application — required fields

Keep the first application short enough to complete on a phone.

### Identity and contact

1. Full name
2. Confirm: “I am 18 or older.”
3. City, state/province, and country
4. Primary email address or mobile number
5. Preferred private contact channel
6. Social handle, optional and not used in selection

### Goal and readiness

7. What physical change are you trying to make over the next 12 weeks, and why does it matter now?
8. What has repeatedly stopped you before?
9. Give one recent example of following through on a difficult commitment for at least eight weeks.
10. Describe your current training, equipment/gym access, and the days you can reliably train.
11. What predictable work, travel, family, or schedule constraint must your plan survive?
12. Are you currently working with another fitness coach? If yes, explain. Current active paid coaching makes an applicant ineligible while that relationship continues.

### Required acknowledgments

13. Confirm: “If selected, I can complete 12 weeks and submit honest weekly check-ins and private progress documentation.”
14. Safety gate: “Do you know of any injury, symptom, diagnosis, medication, pregnancy, recent procedure, or clinician instruction that could affect exercise or nutrition coaching?” Answer yes / no / prefer to discuss privately. Do not give details here.
15. Confirm: “I will complete PKFIT’s private health screen and obtain medical clearance if required before training begins.”
16. Type your full name under this statement:

> I understand this is a 12-week coaching commitment, not a guaranteed result or cash prize. If selected, I will complete onboarding, communicate honestly, submit private weekly check-ins, execute the agreed plan within my safety constraints, and follow the participation terms.

## Separate optional marketing consent

Place this after the application and leave it unchecked. It does not affect selection:

> I separately agree to receive PKFIT fitness and coaching updates by [email or SMS—use one channel-specific choice]. This is not required to apply. I can unsubscribe using the stated method.

Do not bundle email and SMS consent. Application contact data is otherwise used only to administer TWO SEATS.

## Shortlist follow-up — collect privately

Move these deeper questions out of the public form and ask only shortlisted applicants:

1. Describe how you currently manage food and nutrition.
2. What would be different in your daily life if you kept this commitment?
3. When you miss a target, what do you normally do next?
4. What kind of coaching feedback is hardest for you to hear?
5. What are you willing to change for 12 weeks?
6. What are you not willing or able to change?
7. On a scale of 1–10, how confident are you that you can complete weekly check-ins? Explain your number.
8. Can you provide consistent private baseline and weekly progress photos using the same general setup?
9. Is there any privacy concern PKFIT needs to handle during private onboarding or coaching?

Emergency contact information is collected only after provisional selection through approved private onboarding.

## Automatic ineligibility gates

- Under 18
- Currently an active paid PKFIT coaching client
- Cannot participate for the full 12 weeks
- Refuses required health screening or indicated medical clearance
- Cannot access the minimum equipment needed for any safe program PKFIT can provide
- Expects diagnosis, rehabilitation, clinical nutrition, drug guidance, or guaranteed outcomes
- Provides materially false information
- Will not complete weekly check-ins and private coaching documentation


## Human-review flags, not automatic rejection

- Injury, medical condition, medication, pregnancy, or recent procedure
- Major travel or shift-work constraints
- Limited equipment
- Prior program non-completion
- Privacy limitations requiring private operational review
- Financial hardship

These require fit and safety review. They must not be treated as moral failures.

## Ten-minute commitment screen

Ask every shortlisted applicant the same core questions:

1. Walk me through a normal weekday. Where exactly will training happen?
2. What is the first predictable event that could knock you off plan?
3. What will you do when that event happens?
4. Who in your environment supports or resists this change?
5. What does honest weekly documentation look like for you?
6. If progress is slower than expected for three weeks, what will you do?
7. What would make you disappear instead of communicate?
8. Can you complete onboarding and baseline requirements by the stated deadline?
9. Is there any safety or privacy concern we need to handle privately before a decision?
10. Repeat the standard: 12 weeks, private weekly check-ins, honest execution, safety boundaries, and no guaranteed result. Do you agree?

## Screen evaluator note

Listen for specificity, not charisma. Strong applicants describe where, when, how, and what happens after a miss. Weak readiness sounds like intensity without a schedule, total dependence on motivation, or promises of perfection.
