# PKFIT TWO SEATS Site Design Contract

## Mode

Persuade without giveaway spectacle.

## Screen job

Let a Louisiana adult understand the sponsored coaching commitment, safety boundary, selection standard, and submit one minimized application.

## Primary action

Submit the TWO SEATS application.

## Content hierarchy

1. Two seats; the coaching fee is sponsored in full; the standard is not.
2. Exact qualification and deadline.
3. Covered/excluded scope, selection, and safety.
4. Application evidence and commitments.
5. Terms and privacy.

## Visual language

- Existing PKFIT world: near-black training floor, real steel, bone text, one restrained brass/gold signal.
- Typography: Archivo Black declarations and Archivo body.
- Hero: real empty-rack source; generated motion is only a restrained extension of that real source.
- Structure: a training-floor split—offer and physical environment held beside the application.
- No transformation montage, influencer treatment, giveaway confetti, fake result, generic pricing cards, or bright gradient.

## States

- Open application.
- Deadline-closed application with disabled fields and explicit notice.
- Browser-native required-field errors.
- Dedicated receipt state with no implied selection.
- Health details deferred to private finalist onboarding.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: fixed training-floor field with independently scrolling application.
- Mobile: visual opening becomes a bounded 72vh field; application follows in document order.
- Reduced motion: video is hidden and the source photograph remains.

## Evidence used

- PKFIT written voice.
- Campaign final decisions, application contract, consent outline, and accelerated runbook.
- Real campaign rack photograph.
- UIZZE public catalogue browsing was unavailable during this bounded build; no external layout was copied.

## Acceptance criteria

- Exact dates and Louisiana/18+ eligibility are visible.
- “The coaching fee is sponsored in full” is used; incidental costs are not implied covered.
- No detailed health data is requested publicly.
- Active-client conflict, private weekly check-ins, optional public media, separate media consent, and health-screen agreement are explicit.
- Terms, privacy, success, disabled/closed, responsive, focus, and reduced-motion states exist.
