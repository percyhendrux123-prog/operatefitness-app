# TWO SEATS

**Private campaign package. Nothing in this folder is published or live.**

## Recommended campaign

PKFIT selects two serious adults for 12 weeks of coaching with the coaching fee sponsored in full.

**Core line:** I’ll cover the coaching. You bring the standard.

It is merit-selected, not random. Follower count and popularity do not matter. Participants complete an application, standardized commitment screen, and health/onboarding gate before a seat becomes final. Private coaching documentation is weekly. Public media is optional, unscored, and requires a later separate, specific release.
