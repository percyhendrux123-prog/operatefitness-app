# PKFIT TWO SEATS — Accelerated Launch and Onboarding Runbook

**Prepared:** September 13, 2026  
**State:** Private execution script. Publication, applicant contact, selection notices, and client enrollment remain separately approval-gated.

## Outcome

Publish the tested application today, select two people by Tuesday evening, and complete every required onboarding gate by Wednesday, September 16 at 6:00 p.m. CDT. Coaching starts Monday, September 21.

## Calendar

### Sunday, September 13 — launch

1. Finish and independently review page, rules, privacy, form, Reel, Story, and caption.
2. Obtain one exact approval naming the production deploy and each public asset/account.
3. Deploy the form, submit a synthetic test, verify the private Netlify record, delete the test record, then publish approved assets.
4. Record the live URL, exact public copy, publication IDs, and opening timestamp.
5. Do not enable automated DMs, comments, applicant messages, or marketing enrollment.

### Monday, September 14 — intake

1. Check aggregate form count and technical health without moving applicant details into the acquisition scoreboard.
2. Publish at most one approved reminder that adds qualification clarity rather than manufactured pressure.
3. At 8:00 p.m. CDT, close the application in the public UI and use Netlify timestamps to reject late entries.
4. Freeze/export the response set into the approved private review workspace; assign candidate IDs before scoring.

### Tuesday, September 15 — decision

- 7:00–10:00 a.m.: eligibility pass and blinded first scoring.
- 10:00 a.m.–3:00 p.m.: identical ten-minute commitment screens for the six-person shortlist.
- By 4:00 p.m.: independent second review of finalists and close scores; conflicts recused.
- By 5:00 p.m.: Percy approves two provisional selections and ranked alternates.
- After approval only: send the exact provisional-selection and alternate messages one person at a time with readback.

### Wednesday, September 16 — onboarding

Each provisional selection must complete by 6:00 p.m. CDT:

1. private full intake and PAR-Q/approved health screen;
2. any indicated medical clearance;
3. PKFIT informed-consent/coaching terms;
4. emergency contact and approved communication channel;
5. Coachapp account creation and access verification;
6. baseline instructions and first check-in date.

After the seat is final, offer a separate optional media release. Declining it has no effect on selection, retention, coaching, or the sponsored fee.

If a gate remains incomplete at 6:00 p.m., the seat is not final. Percy may approve the next eligible alternate. Never waive or backdate a gate.

## Non-compressible stop rules

- A safety flag is reviewed; it is not scored as weakness.
- No program begins before required clearance.
- No selected person becomes a client merely because a message says “selected.”
- No public identity or progress asset is required. Use one only after separate, specific written media consent and a separate publication approval.
- No applicant data enters a marketing list.
- No scoring decision uses follower count, appearance, hardship narrative, social identity choice, or health status.
- If fewer than two applicants safely clear every gate, start with fewer than two. The number is not more important than the standard.

## Retention execution

Within 30 days after the final selection decision or campaign close—whether zero, one, or two seats are filled—an authorized operator deletes non-selected responses from Netlify Forms and removes derived exports from the restricted review workspace. Record the action in a private deletion log and verify that working copies and trash locations no longer contain the records. Move only the minimum selected-participant data needed for coaching into the approved client system, then remove the campaign copy within the same window.

## Capacity boundary

Two seats are the maximum. The challenge calendar does not absorb campaign onboarding after September 16; unresolved candidates move to hold/alternate rather than creating a cluster.
