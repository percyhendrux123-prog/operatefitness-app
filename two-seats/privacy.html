Privacy notice

This notice applies only to the PKFIT TWO SEATS application.

What the public form collects

Name, adult and Louisiana confirmation, city, one chosen private contact route, readiness answers, schedule and training access, current-coach status, public identity preference, required acknowledgments, and a yes/no/prefer-private safety gate.

What not to submit

Do not submit detailed diagnoses, medication details, medical records, government identification, payment information, passwords, private client records, or financial information. Detailed health information is collected only from provisional finalists through the approved private onboarding system.

Use and access

Application information is used only to administer eligibility, review, selection, and onboarding for TWO SEATS. It will not be added to a general marketing list. There is no bundled email or SMS marketing consent. Access is limited to authorized campaign reviewers and coaching personnel with a need to know.

Selection privacy

Candidate IDs are assigned before first-pass scoring. Optional social information is not requested by this form. Unnecessary identity details are withheld from first-pass reviewers. Health flags and public identity preference are not scoring factors.

Retention

Non-selected application records are scheduled for deletion 30 days after both seats are finalized, except when a dispute, security issue, or legal-preservation need requires a limited hold. Selected-client records move only into the approved private coaching system and follow its retention rules.

Media

This application is not the final media release. Selected participants complete a separate written media consent defining identity format, allowed content, approved channels, truthful editing, and exclusions. Private health details are excluded by default.

Questions

Contact command@operatefitness.app. Do not email health records or other sensitive documents.
