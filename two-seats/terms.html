TWO SEATS
campaign terms

Applications open only after the public launch is approved and close Monday, September 14, 2026 at 8:00 p.m. CDT. Netlify’s recorded submission time governs. Late or incomplete submissions are not considered.

Sponsor and contact

PKFIT, operated by Percy Keith. Campaign questions: command@operatefitness.app.

Offer

Two sponsored seats are available. Each selected participant receives twelve weeks of PKFIT coaching with the coaching fee sponsored in full, beginning Monday, September 21, 2026 and ending Sunday, December 13, 2026. The sponsored benefit has no cash value and cannot be transferred.

Eligibility

  • Louisiana resident and 18 or older.
  • Not an active paid PKFIT coaching client.
  • Able to participate for twelve weeks with practical training access.
  • Willing to complete private health screening, obtain medical clearance when indicated, submit weekly check-ins, and complete the disclosed public progress checkpoints under separate media consent.

Selection

No purchase is required. This is not a random drawing. Selection is based on eligibility, readiness, coachability, goal clarity, schedule and environment, training access, and practical ability to complete the program. Follower count, popularity, hardship narrative, preferred identity format, and health status are not scored. Conflicts require reviewer recusal. Close finalists receive independent review.

Conditions

A provisional selection is not final until the applicant completes the private health screen, any indicated medical clearance, informed-consent and coaching terms, separate media consent, emergency contact, communication setup, and Coachapp access by Wednesday, September 16, 2026 at 6:00 p.m. CDT. If a required gate is incomplete, PKFIT may offer the seat to the next eligible alternate. A safety limitation is reviewed, not treated as a moral or scoring failure.

Scope and exclusions

The coaching includes individual training structure, general nutrition coaching within PKFIT’s lawful scope, weekly check-ins, progress tracking, adjustments, and normal in-app communication. Gym access, food, equipment, healthcare, travel, and optional supplements are not included. PKFIT is not an emergency, diagnostic, rehabilitation, or clinical service.

Results and conduct

No result is guaranteed. Participants must provide truthful intake information, communicate material limitations, execute safely, and stop or seek appropriate care when symptoms or qualified professional guidance require it. False information, unsafe attributed practices, or persistent noncommunication may end participation under the written notice process.

Documentation

Private weekly documentation is required. Four limited public checkpoints—baseline, Week 4, Week 8, and Week 12—are required under a separate written media consent. A participant may use a full name, first name, alias, or anonymous identity. Private health details and private weekly submissions are excluded unless separately approved.

Privacy

Application information is used for campaign administration only. See the privacy notice. PKFIT may cancel, pause, or modify the campaign only for operational, legal, safety, platform, or force-majeure reasons, and will not silently change the terms for submitted applicants.
