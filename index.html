FREQUENTLY ASKED

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN LITE, SYSTEM AND STANDARD?

LITE is a $20 one-time set of tools: your calories, your training and cardio numbers. SYSTEM is a $47 one-time plan built from your intake, with a log for every set and meal and the option to connect your own AI. Standard is $250 per month with a coach: personalized programming, weekly check-ins read by a person, and video form review.

WHAT DOES PKFIT SYSTEM INCLUDE?

A seven-day training week with real exercises, sets and reps. Calorie, protein, fat and carb targets from your numbers, with a protein floor the app enforces. A weekly meal structure from the PKFIT library. A rest timer, last-time ghost text, day swaps, a weekly check-in and progress trends. Optional connection to your own Claude or ChatGPT, read-only or full control of your own plan. No coach reviews it.

WHO SHOULD NOT BUY SYSTEM?

Anyone who answers yes on the health screen; the purchase stops before payment and points to coaching. Anyone in contest prep. Anyone who needs to be held to the plan; that is what Standard is for.

DOES PKFIT LITE INCLUDE 1:1 COACHING?

No. LITE does not include 1:1 coaching, direct messages, or weekly coach adjustments.

IS PKFIT LITE A SUBSCRIPTION?

No. PKFIT LITE is a one-time $20 purchase.

WHAT IS OPERATE FITNESS?

Operate Fitness is a branded coaching app for coaches. It brings programs, nutrition, check-ins, messaging, resources, community, and challenges into one coach-owned delivery layer.
